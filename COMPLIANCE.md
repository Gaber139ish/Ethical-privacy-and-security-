# Compliance Overview

This is not legal advice. This section tracks engineering controls mapped to common requirements.

## GDPR/CCPA Mapping (selected)
- Lawful basis and consent: captured alongside data collection
- Data subject rights: documented DSR workflow and SLAs
- Purpose limitation: tagged processing purposes, policy checks in pipelines
- Data minimization and retention: defaults + documented schedules
- Security: encryption at rest/in transit; access controls; audit logs
- DPIA: maintain for high-risk processing
